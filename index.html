Accessibility & privacy

Design

Make text legible, use larger tap targets, and keep controls keyboard-friendly for a smoother experience.

Photos

Always get consent before accepting photos and add moderation safeguards if guests can upload content.

Data

If you collect emails or RSVP details, clearly explain where the information is stored and how it is used.
